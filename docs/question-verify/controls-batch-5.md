#### VC-029 — SOUND
citation: none — technique/vehicle layout, not law
note: NRTR reg 150 is the only motorcycle-control-adjacent provision and it does **not** prescribe hand/foot placement — it only requires two independent braking systems: "not equipped with two independent braking systems, one of which shall act.on the front wheel or wheels and the other which shall act on the rear wheel or wheels". Do **not** cite reg 150 in support of this item; it would be a misattribution. The left-lever/left-foot layout is the ISO/industry standard the K53 manual teaches, nothing more.

#### VC-030 — SOUND
citation: none — braking technique, not law. "anti-lock" occurs exactly once in the NRTR (reg on lamp colours, "a brake anti-lock warning light to the front of a trailer") and is irrelevant here. There is no SA regulation stating what ABS does.
note: the explanation's "it can lengthen it on gravel" is correct engineering consensus (locked wheels build a wedge on loose surfaces) but it is **convention, not a sourced SA claim** — if the accuracy gate wants a citation for it, there isn't one. It is not asserted as law, so it stands.

#### VC-031 — SOUND
citation: NRTR reg 203
quote: "203. No person shall operate on a public road a motor vehicle with a windscreen which is not fitted with at least one windscreen wiper which shall be capable of operation by other than manual means and shall, when in operation, wipe the outside of the windscreen directly in front of the driver, continuously, evenly and adequately: Provided that the provisions of this regulation shall not apply to a motor cycle, a motor tricycle or a motor quadrucycle without a fixed hood."
note: this is the only genuine citation in the batch, and it corroborates the explanation's exact wording ("the outside of the windscreen"). The explanation asserts nothing beyond it. Attacked the distractors: hazard lights and hooter do nothing for forward visibility; and although rain often coincides with **inside** misting (which needs the demister, not the wiper), no demister option is offered and the stem attributes the blur to the rain, so the item is not ambiguous.

#### VC-032 — EXPLANATION_DEFECT
citation: none — transmission mechanics, not law ("clutch" / "automatic transmission" return zero hits in the NRTR)
issue: the key option and the explanation both teach "an automatic has **no clutch**". That is mechanically false — a torque-converter automatic contains a lock-up clutch and multiple clutch packs, and a dual-clutch automatic has two. What is true is that it has **no clutch pedal / no driver-operated clutch**. The marked answer is still the only defensible one of the three (distractor 2 "no gears at all" is wrong for any conventional automatic, and only arguably true of a CVT — well outside learner scope), so this is a wording fix, not a wrong answer: change the option to "An automatic has no clutch pedal and selects gears itself" and the explanation's "It is not clutch-operated" to "There is no clutch pedal to operate."
