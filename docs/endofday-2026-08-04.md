### Technical notes (dev team)

- **Provenance is now honest.** `questions.approved_by` became `text`; the 228 AI-swept rows read `'system'` with `verified_at` null, the 46 human sign-offs carry a user id and a timestamp. Do not backfill `verified_at` — `verified_at is null` *is* the worklist. Third `review_status` value `withdrawn` added (migration `20260804100000`); learner getters and RLS already filter on `= 'approved'`, so serving is unaffected.
- **The sign-code guard was over-scoped** and blocked *editing* any question whose sign had been withdrawn, not just approving it — that was Louwrens's blocker. Now scoped to approval only.
- **Signs served 356** (340 signs + 16 markings). `R1` was `review_status: draft` while `R1.1` served under the name "Stop sign"; SADC RTSM Vol 1 §2.2.1 confirms R1 is primary and R1.1 a derivative for restricted-visibility urban sites.
- **Citations:** 232 → 267 of 274, read out of `resources/` with the governing sentence quoted in each repair file's `why`. **Regs 149 and 213 were amended by GNR.846 of 31 Oct 2014 — later than our consolidated copy (GNR.209 of 2012)** — reg 149 was substituted wholesale and the first pass quoted superseded wording. Check the 2014 gazette as well as the consolidation before citing anything. Sixteen citations carry a `NOTE FOR THE HUMAN VERIFIER` where the source supports the item but not the keyed answer.
- **Repeat suppression:** `assemblePaper` takes the last 2 completed papers and draws from what the learner hasn't just seen; two-tier, with a deterministic least-recently-seen top-up. Simulated at live pool sizes, paper-over-paper repeats 24% → 0%.
- **Claims audit** in `docs/claims-audit-2026-08-04.md`. Two things it could not fix in copy: six served signs carry no recorded verification evidence, and **only `/mock` is entitlement-gated** — practice, explanations and the whole library are already free, so R179 currently buys mock exams and the AI assessment. That is a product decision, not a wording bug. The price now interpolates from `lib/pricing.ts` instead of sitting as a literal in two locales.
- **`shuffle`/`shuffleOptions` extracted to `src/lib/shuffle.ts`** — `exam.ts` and `readiness-sample.ts` had each grown their own Fisher-Yates. `shuffleOptions` now throws on an out-of-range answer index rather than silently producing `answer: -1`; `saveQuestion` guards that on write but the data-repair files bypass it via PostgREST.
- **Practice mode still renders stored option order** — same positional bias, and it is the surface with the most repetition. One `.map(shuffleOptions)` away.
- Three adversarial reviews run (Grok / GPT-5.6 / Gemini). Worth recording: one model raised a confident HIGH asserting NRTR reg 304 governs unattended vehicles and reg 305 the stopping prohibitions. The text says the opposite — 304 *is* "Stopping of vehicles" and 304(h) is verbatim our cited rule. It cited from recall. That is the failure the accuracy gate exists to prevent, produced by a verification tool, and it is the argument for the human pass above.
