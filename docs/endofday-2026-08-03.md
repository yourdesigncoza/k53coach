## What we did today

| Update | Details |
| -- | -- |
| **Paying for access now works, all the way through** | Until today we could send a learner to the payment page, but we had never proved the last step: the payment provider telling us the money arrived, and the app unlocking automatically. We put a full test purchase through and it worked unaided — access was granted straight away, for exactly 90 days. We also checked the things that cost money when they go wrong: a learner who already paid can't accidentally pay twice, and if the provider sends the same confirmation twice we don't grant two separate accesses. This was the last untested part of the payment path. |
| **Removed 13 road signs that were showing up blank** | These are the signs whose whole meaning is written *on* them — "30 min" parking, pay-and-display, time bands, arrows pointing left or right. We had the coloured border and box drawn correctly, but the writing and arrows inside were missing. A blank one isn't a rough version of the sign, it's the sign with its meaning removed, so they've been pulled until we have proper artwork. |
| **Removed 3 signs that don't exist in South Africa** | Found while checking artwork. One was a European sign we'd picked up by mistake. Teaching a learner a sign that isn't on the test is worse than not teaching it. |
| **Corrected 7 "end of restriction" signs** | These tell you a limit no longer applies. Ours were drawn with the wrong marker through them — close enough to look right at a glance, wrong enough to teach the wrong thing. |
| **Checked every sign picture against the official chart** | 93 signs had been sourced from the web without being compared to the official Department of Transport chart. We've now put each one side by side with the real chart entry. Most were fine; the problems above are what that check found. The library is now 352 signs, down from 375 — smaller, but every one of them verified. |
| **Wrote 48 new questions** | 41 road-sign questions and 7 on temporary roadworks signs, plus a new lesson on temporary signs, which we had no coverage of at all. |
| **Checked the vehicle-control lessons against the official manual** | Went through the "controls of the vehicle" lessons line by line against the Department of Transport's own learner manual. Mostly confirmed what we had. We did cut one claim about traffic lights that the manual doesn't actually support — we'd rather teach less than teach something we can't stand behind. |
| **Fixed a batch of content errors** | Around 40 corrections across signs and questions — a sign described the wrong way round, arrows read backwards, and a group of signs whose value changes by location (speed limits, weight limits) being taught as if the number were always the same. |
| **The free test now really is 5 questions** | The front page promised "5 questions, 30 seconds" and the test was actually 15. It now shows 5, drawn in the same mix as the real exam, and picks a different 5 each time so a learner retaking it gets a fresh set. |
| **Reworded explanations that read like legal documents** | A few answer explanations quoted regulation numbers at the learner. Accuracy stays exactly where it was — it's recorded behind the scenes — but what the learner reads is now about what to do at the wheel. |
| **Questions can no longer be approved without a source** | The admin screen now refuses to mark a question approved unless a reference to the official source is filled in, and records who approved it and when. |
| **Built an automated site check** | A script that drives the real site like a learner would — front page, free test, lessons, paywall, purchase — in about a minute. It's what we'll run before every release instead of clicking through by hand. |
