## Things that will bite whoever picks this up

**Our Regulations copy is the 2000 original.** Two items turned on amendments — and one was a trap:
**reg 101(1) was never textually amended**, so it still reads *18 months* everywhere. The extension
to 24 came by ministerial notice *under* it (GN 134, GG 32959). A reviewer following the reg text
alone will "refute" a correct answer forever. Every duration, fee and age carries this exposure.
Amending gazettes now in `init/` (gitignored).

**Foreign signage is the recurring failure mode.** Five separate items taught UK/US/European
conventions as South African — the two-diagonal no-stopping sign, the blue pedestrian crossing, the
yellow diamond warning, triangles as a yield line, a blue one-way sign. They survive review because
they *feel* like general road knowledge. Check every sign claim against Schedule 1 or
`data/verify/verdicts/<CODE>.json`, never against recall.

**A blank citation is a result, not a gap.** 26 items should stay uncited. There is no provision on
head-restraint height, demisting or ABS. This bank already shipped five drafts citing `NRTA s 4(3)`,
which does not exist — a fabricated citation is worse than none because it defeats the audit trail.

**Don't trust a summary over the source.** Several claims made today were wrong and only caught by
going back to the primary text — including my own: "zero guidance-class assets" (there are 26),
`RS-023 → W105` (it is W104, W105 is the *skew* T-junction), and `RS-020`'s "information sign"
(R305-P is regulatory).
