Road markings — redraw for review

Left = shipped (chart vignette). Right = redraw, drawn to the SARTSM specification. Nothing is wired up — see README.md. K53-37 · 2026-08-04

RM1 · No Overtaking Line
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.5
White, continuous, minimum 100 mm wide, down the centre of a two-way road.
RM2 · No Crossing Lines
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.6
Two continuous solid white lines, each minimum 100 mm wide.
RM4.1 · Left Edge Line
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.8; NRTR 2000 reg 298A(1)-(2)
Continuous solid YELLOW line, minimum 100 mm wide, marking the LEFT edge of the roadway. Centre line shown broken for context.
RM5 · Painted Island
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.9
White boundary lines minimum 100 mm wide enclosing yellow sloping bars, minimum 150 mm wide, bar-to-space ratio 1:2.
RM6 · Parking Bays
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.10; NRTR 2000 reg 305
White lines minimum 100 mm wide defining the limits of each bay. Driver must park wholly within them.
RM7 · Exclusive Parking Bay
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.11 (RM7, RM7.1)
Bay demarcated by a continuous solid YELLOW line on three sides, carrying oval marking RM7.1 with a designatory letter.
RM8 · Mandatory Direction Arrows
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.12
YELLOW arrows (variants RM8.1–RM8.6) marked in a lane on the approach to a junction. The arrow in YOUR lane is the one that binds.
RM9 · Exclusive Use Lane Line
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.13, §7.2.21; NRTR 2000 reg 289
Broken YELLOW line minimum 150 mm wide, used together with an exclusive-use word or symbol marking (RM17).
RM10 · Box Junction
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.14
Continuous YELLOW boundary lines enclosing yellow cross-hatched diagonals, all minimum 100 mm wide.
RM12 · No Stopping Line
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.16(2); paired sign R217 per §2.4.14.4; NRTR 2000 reg 304
TWO forms. Continuous solid RED, minimum 150 mm wide, applies 24 hours. Broken RED, minimum 100 mm wide, applies only during the periods on the accompanying sign. Both forms shown — one on each kerb.
RM13 · No Parking Line
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.17(2); paired sign R216 per §2.4.13.4
TWO forms. Continuous solid YELLOW, minimum 100 mm wide, applies 24 hours. Broken YELLOW, minimum 100 mm wide, applies only during the periods on the accompanying sign. Both forms shown — one on each kerb.
RM15 · Traffic Circle Mandatory Direction Arrows
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.19
Marked in YELLOW, in SETS OF THREE equally spaced arrows, around a mini-circle. Drivers may proceed only in the direction indicated.
RTM1 · Stop line
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.1(4); NRTR 2000 reg 286(2)(c)(i), reg 307
Continuous solid WHITE line, minimum 300 mm wide urban / 500 mm rural, across the full width of the approach. Deliberately NO 'STOP' legend — that is a separate word marking, and the chart vignette's legend was chart context.
RTM2 · Yield line
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.2(4); NRTR 2000 reg 286(2)(c)(ii)
BROKEN white line, minimum 300 mm wide urban / 500 mm rural, line-to-gap ratio 2:1 (600 mm line, 300 mm gap recommended).
RTM3 · Pedestrian crossing lines
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.3(2); NRTR 2000 reg 315
TWO continuous white lines, each minimum 100 mm wide, at least 2,4 m apart (3 m preferred), across the full width of the roadway.
RTM4 · Block pedestrian crossing
SARTSM Vol 1 §7.2.4(2); NRTR 2000 reg 286(2)(c)(iii), reg 315
Rectangular white blocks, minimum 2,4 m long and 600 mm wide, spaced 600 mm apart, across the full width of the roadway. Blocks run WITH the direction of travel.
