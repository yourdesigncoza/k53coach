# restricted/ — NOT a permitted content source

## Read this before opening anything in this folder

These are circulated **"memo" scans of live licence-test terminals** — four papers
(A, B, C, D). They are here because they are the only evidence we have of what the real
computerised test actually looks like. They are **not** ours, and they are **not** a
source.

The files themselves are gitignored and must never reach the remote. Only this README is
tracked.

## What they may be used for

**Format reference only.** The structural facts confirmed from these scans — and nothing
else — are recorded in `docs/exam-format-analysis/`:

- the paper is **64 questions**, not 68
- **3 options** per question, not 4
- question order is **shuffled**
- **road markings are examined**

Those are facts about the exam's shape. Facts are not copyrightable and the derived
metadata in `docs/exam-format-analysis/` is the tracked, usable output.

## What they must never be used for

- ❌ **Never a content source.** Do not copy, adapt, reword or "take inspiration from" a
  question, an answer, a distractor or an explanation in these scans. Every question in
  our bank is drafted from the NRTA, the NRTR and the official chart — see
  `resources/README.md`.
- ❌ **Never passed to `llmChat` or any drafting prompt.** That is exactly where
  regurgitation of protected expression happens. Same rule as the commercial study guide
  in `CLAUDE.md` constraint 7.
- ❌ **Never committed, deployed, or shared with the client or Linear.**

## Why this matters

Content is the moat (`CLAUDE.md` constraint 4). A product sold on being *accurate* and
*original* cannot have a question bank traceable to someone else's paper. The commercial
value and the legal position both rest on our items being written from the primary law.
